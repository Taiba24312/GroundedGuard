passage 1: The Riverside Public Library opened in 1998 in the former town hall on Elm Street. Its collection holds about 120,000 books, including a local-history archive of maps and newspapers going back to 1870.

passage 2: The library is open Monday to Saturday from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. Library cards are free for residents, and members can borrow up to 15 items at a time for three weeks.
